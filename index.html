FAQ

Quick answers.

What do I get?

A 15-second vertical (9:16) cinematic commercial for your business, ready for Instagram Reels, TikTok, YouTube Shorts and Stories.

What is the preview?

A short first look at your concept and style before you order the full spot. It’s $39, or free with the 60-second preview promo.

Do I need to film anything?

No. Tell me about your business and the vibe you want. Photos of your space, products or logo help guide the look.

Is it really made with AI?

Yes. The visuals are generated with AI, then edited, color graded, scored and finished with on-screen text.
